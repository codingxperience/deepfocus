`DeepFocus v3.dc.html` — the rework, built on one idea: **the Week Sheet**.

Every week of every course is one page, in one scroll, printable: `01 Objective` → `02 Textbook` → `03 Prepare` → `04 Practise` → `05 Teach one another` → `06 This sheet in your grade`. A course is a bound set of those sheets with a visible binder edge; the shelf is your semester. The textbook reference sits in a strip above everything, and it always names the exact chapter for the sheet you're on, not the book.

What's real rather than decorative:

- **Email decides the desk.** `@student.*` → learner, `@staff.*` → instructor, `@admin.*` → registry. No role switch anywhere; the People screen states the rule as policy, since changing what someone can do means issuing a different address.
- **Grades logic.** Four groups — Prepare 25, Practise 30, Teach one another 20, Final 25 — weights editable by the instructor with a save gate that refuses anything but 100%, and the learner sees that same table with their own marks and the contribution arithmetic. The graded-only toggle is a genuine what-if: off, everything outstanding counts as zero.
- **Teach one another** is three graded types, as you asked: rostered teach, teach-back with two peer acknowledgements, and group units presented at the gathering — with a 14-week rota published four weeks ahead.
- **Pharmacology I** is authored across 14 sheets on Open RN *Nursing Pharmacology 2e*, a CC BY 4.0 open textbook for prelicensure nursing students, aligned to the WTCS nursing pharmacology curriculum, cross-checked against the Ministry of Health *Uganda Clinical Guidelines 2023*, plus OpenStax, WHO's electronic essential medicines list and DailyMed. AI literacy is Sheet 02 only, as you chose. Library carries all six English strands.

Two things I did deliberately against instinct, and will change if you disagree: rather than invent stock photo URLs, course covers use your six real photos plus verified Wikimedia Commons files, each with a fallback so a dead link degrades to a tinted cover instead of a hole; and where a single YouTube video couldn't be guaranteed to still exist, the link is a prepared search, labelled as one — I won't publish a link I haven't confirmed. Old versions are untouched in `DeepFocus.dc.html` and `DeepFocus v2.dc.html`.
